Our Story

A Local Shop, Built on Word of Mouth

SSAC — Sri Sai AirControls — started with a simple goal: help homes and businesses stay comfortably cool without the usual hassle of chasing down dealers and technicians.

What began as a small local shop has grown through honest work and repeat customers. Today we sell, install, service, and repair air conditioners of all major brands—and we stand behind every job we do, big or small.

Whether you need a brand-new split AC fitted in a single room or ongoing maintenance across an entire office, our team shows up, explains the problem in plain language, and gets it fixed.
