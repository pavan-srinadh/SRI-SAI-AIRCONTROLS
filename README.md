# ❄️ SSAC — Sri Sai AirControls

> **Complete Cooling Solutions — AC Sales · Installation · Service · Repair**

SSAC (Sri Sai AirControls) is a professional air-conditioning service website designed to provide customers with an easy way to explore AC services, request quotations, contact technicians, and connect directly through phone or WhatsApp.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://srisaiaircontrols.netlify.app/

---

## 📌 About the Project

The SSAC website is a modern, responsive business website developed for **Sri Sai AirControls**, an AC sales and service provider.

The website helps customers:

* Explore AC installation services
* Request AC repair and troubleshooting
* Get gas refilling and leak-fixing services
* Learn about annual maintenance services
* Explore commercial and office cooling solutions
* View completed AC installation and service work
* Contact the business through phone or WhatsApp
* Request a free service estimate

The website focuses on a clean, professional design with clear calls-to-action and mobile-friendly navigation.
